Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Effective 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Ygor app (formerly Krugozore AI) for iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Mac, and in section 11 to the ygor.app website and its update emails

Ygor has no account, no login and no server of its own. Every AI model in Ygor runs on your device, so your chats and prompts stay there. The app goes online only to download models you choose, and, if you leave them on, for the small lookups of Weather and Web search described below.

Who is responsible

Ygor is developed and published by Nikolay Gushchin, an independent developer based in Zurich, Switzerland. Questions about this policy: support@ygor.app.

The Ygor app for iPhone and iPad (formerly Krugozore AI) is not related to the Telegram bot named Krugozore AI. The two are separate products with different architectures, ideas and technology stacks; nothing described on this page applies to the bot, and nothing the bot does applies to this app.

What Ygor collects

Nothing. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server that could receive your chats, your prompts, your model choices or your usage, and therefore does not receive them.

This is about the app. The ygor.app website counts page views without cookies and offers an optional email list; both are described in section 11 and have nothing to do with the app.

What stays on your device

  1. Your chats are saved as files in Ygor's own storage on your device, together with the photos, audio and files you attach. For some documents, Ygor keeps the text it extracted rather than the original file.
  2. My Flex progress and your 👍/👎 reply ratings are saved on your device too.
  3. Models you download are stored on your device.
  4. Nothing syncs automatically. A chat or file leaves your device through Export or Share only when you choose to send it somewhere.
  5. Profile › Data › Delete all my data erases everything the app stored, including anything it kept in the iOS Keychain. Deleting the app removes its files too.

When you ask a model

  1. Every model in this version runs on your device: Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence model, or an open model you downloaded. Your prompts, the conversation and anything you attach are processed on your device and are not sent to any AI provider.
  2. Voice input is transcribed by Apple's speech recognition on your device; Ygor uses it only where on-device recognition is available. A voice message is held in memory while it is processed; the recording is not saved in your chat files.
  3. The Weather and Web search tools, if you leave them on, make their own small lookups (clause 5.1 and 5.2). They send a place name or a short query the model writes, never your message or the conversation.
  4. Chats from an earlier version may contain replies from cloud models, including free models that may have used your prompts, photos, documents and audio for training. In those chats, the chat's shield still says so.

Network requests

  1. Weather. Only the place name goes to nominatim.openstreetmap.org (OpenStreetMap) to find it, then that place's coordinates go to api.open-meteo.com for the forecast. Never your conversation, never your device's location.
  2. Web search. A short query the assistant writes goes to Parallel Search (search.parallel.ai), without an account and without your message or the conversation. The same applies when Ygor looks for pictures on the web because no image model can run on your device: nothing is sent until you tap Search, and the results are shown as link cards built from the search response, with no images loaded from the sites found. If a Parallel key was entered in a test build, it is kept only in the iOS Keychain, sent only with these searches, and removed by Delete all my data.
  3. Model downloads. Model and tokenizer files, and the list of files that make up a model, are downloaded from huggingface.co when you start a download.

These requests fetch information or files. They don't carry your prompts. Each service named above is run by someone else. It sees an ordinary internet request, including your IP address, and its own policy governs what it keeps.

Purchases

Buying or restoring Ygor Lifetime is handled by Apple through the App Store. Ygor checks the purchase on your device using StoreKit and stores no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Links and mail

Links you tap, such as a web search result or the acknowledgements, open that website in Safari because you asked to. Send feedback opens Mail with a message to support@ygor.app that you write and send yourself; the draft lists only the app version, iOS version, device model and language.

Notifications

Reminders, streak notices and reports are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. There is no push server.

Children

Ygor is rated for ages 16 and up on the App Store and is not directed at children.

Your rights

For the app, the developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control. If you joined the update list (section 11), you can ask at support@ygor.app what is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent at any time. If you write to support, your email is used only to answer you; you can ask for it to be deleted at any time. For data held by a service named above, contact that service directly. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Switzerland that is the FDPIC.

The ygor.app website and update emails

This section is about the website, not the app.

  1. Hosting. The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, it receives an ordinary request from your browser, including your IP address, and GitHub's own privacy statement governs what it keeps. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  2. Page counting. When a page of ygor.app opens, it sends one request to our counter: the page's name (home, privacy, support or stats) and the name of the website you came from, sorted into a group such as search engine, LinkedIn or direct. The counter adds one to a tally per day, page and group and keeps nothing else: no cookie, no identifier, no full web addresses, no IP address. Your IP address is used for about a minute, only inside Cloudflare's rate limiter, to stop floods. The tallies are public at ygor.app/stats and are kept for about 400 days.
  3. The update list. If you sign up, we store your email address, which boxes you ticked, the version of the wording you saw (currently 2026-10-26), when you signed up and confirmed, and a hashed form of your confirmation code. “Send me Ygor updates” (required) means emails about new versions, models and features of Ygor. “Also tell me about other products and projects by Nick Gushchin” (optional) means occasional emails about his other work. Your address is used for nothing else and is never sold or shared for anyone else's marketing.
  4. Consent and double opt-in. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR where it applies, and consent under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection). After you sign up, we send one confirmation email, and nothing else arrives until you click its link. The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which loads from Cloudflare only once you start using the form and checks that a person, not a script, is signing up.
  5. Who processes it. The list is stored by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) in its D1 database, located in Western Europe, and the counter runs on Cloudflare Workers. Emails are sent by Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), which receives your address and the message for that purpose. Both act as processors under their data processing terms, and data may be processed in the USA. Our emails carry no tracking pixels and no tracked links.
  6. How long. Until you unsubscribe. Sign-ups that aren't confirmed within 7 days are deleted automatically. When you unsubscribe, everything is deleted except your address and the date, kept only as a suppression record so the address is never emailed again by mistake. Ask, and we delete that too.
  7. Unsubscribing and deletion. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete what is stored, write to support@ygor.app.

None of this involves the app: the app still collects nothing (section 2).

Changes

If a network destination is added, removed or changed, this policy and the in-app Privacy screen (Profile › About › Privacy) change in the same app update. The effective date and version above are updated with each change. Sections and clauses are numbered so an amendment can name exactly what moved, for example "clause 4.3".

Version 1.2 (26 October 2026) added section 11 about the ygor.app website: cookieless page counting and the optional update list. Nothing changed in the app.

Version 1.1 removed the OpenRouter connection: Ygor no longer sends messages to OpenRouter or any cloud model provider, and an OpenRouter key saved by an earlier version is deleted from the Keychain when 1.1 first starts.
