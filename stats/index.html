How it's counted

Each page sends one small request when it opens: the page's name and the name of the website you came from, nothing more. There are no cookies, nothing is stored in your browser, and your IP address is used for a minute only to stop floods and never saved. Visitors who block scripts aren't counted. The update count includes confirmed addresses only. Details in the privacy policy; raw figures: stats as JSON.
