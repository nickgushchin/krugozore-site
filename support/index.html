Support

Ygor (formerly Krugozore AI) for iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Mac · Version 1.1

Write to support@ygor.app. The fastest way is Profile › About › Send feedback inside the app, which opens a mail draft with your app version, iOS version, device model and language already filled in. You write and send it yourself.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account or a key? No. Every model in Ygor runs on your device: Apple Intelligence on iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence turned on, or an open model you download in the Models tab.
  • Do I have to pay for models? No. Models run on your device, so there is no usage bill. Downloading one needs an internet connection and free storage space.
  • What does Lifetime unlock? Unlimited chats and folders and your own Smart Groups. Models, tools, attachments, exports and every privacy feature are included without it.
  • I bought Lifetime on another device. Profile › Ygor Lifetime Deal › Restore Purchases.
  • Does anything leave my device? Model downloads come from huggingface.co. If you leave Weather or Web search on, they send a place name or a short query the assistant writes, never your conversation. Details in the Privacy Policy.
  • Where are my chats? On your device only. Export or share a chat from its menu. Profile › Data › Delete all my data erases everything.
