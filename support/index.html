Support

Krugozore AI for iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Mac

Write to krugozore.ios.app@gmail.com. The fastest way is Profile › About › Send feedback inside the app, which opens a mail draft with your app and iOS version already filled in.

Common questions

  • Where do I get a key? Krugozore uses your own OpenRouter key. In onboarding, tap Connect with OpenRouter, or paste a key you created at openrouter.ai/settings/keys. You can change it any time in Profile › OpenRouter key.
  • Do I have to pay for models? No. Free models cost nothing; filter the Models tab by Free. Paid models are billed by OpenRouter at the maker's price to your own account. Krugozore adds nothing on top.
  • What does Lifetime unlock? Unlimited chats and folders and your own Model Groups. Model usage is not included; that stays with your OpenRouter account.
  • I bought Lifetime on another device. Profile › Krugozore Lifetime Deal › Restore Purchases.
  • Can I use it without any key? Yes, with a model that runs on your device: Apple Intelligence on iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence turned on, or a downloaded on-device model.
  • Where are my chats? On your device only. Export or share a chat from its menu. Profile › Data › Delete all my data erases everything.
